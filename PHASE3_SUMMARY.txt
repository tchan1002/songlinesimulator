SOUND MAPPINGS:
---------------
BUS Routes:
  Route 22 → A# bass tone (220 Hz)
  Route 6  → F# bass tone (185 Hz)
  Duration scales with number of stops

CTA Train Lines:
  Red Line    → C4 (261 Hz)
  Blue Line   → E4 (329 Hz)
  Brown Line  → G4 (392 Hz)
  Green Line  → A4 (440 Hz)
  Orange Line → D4 (293 Hz)
  Purple Line → F4 (349 Hz)
  Yellow Line → B4 (493 Hz)
  Pink Line   → A#4 (466 Hz)
  Rhythmic pulses = number of stops
